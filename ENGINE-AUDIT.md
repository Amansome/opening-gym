# Engine audit

Generated by `node scripts/engine-audit.js` (Stockfish 19 lite, depth 14, MultiPV 5).

- 30 courses, 1872 unique positions analysed, 1204 trained moves checked.
- A trained move is **suspect** when it scores more than 80 cp worse than the engine's best move (mover's view). Only the trained side's moves are audited.
- `fine` moves: other moves within 50 cp of the best move are stored in data/evals.json and marked `fine` in the app (not counted as mistakes).
- Depth 14 with the lite network can misjudge sharp lines (gambits, mating attacks): treat entries as leads to review, not verdicts.
- Moves that occur only in `warning` lines (deliberate mistakes, "trap to avoid") are not audited: 25 such trained move(s) would otherwise have been flagged. In trap collections only the side that wins each line is audited.
- **7 suspect move(s)** in 6 course(s).
